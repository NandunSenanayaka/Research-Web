The Challenge

Fragmented Dental Analysis

Limitations in conventional dental radiograph workflows often stem from treating complex diagnostic steps as isolated processes rather than a continuous patient-centred journey.

Unreliable Inputs

Rotated, mirrored, or blurred images can compromise downstream AI analysis and lead to inaccurate diagnostic results.

Limited Interpretation

Basic localization alone lacks the granular severity information needed for meaningful clinical assessment and care planning.

No Historical Context

Analyzing a single radiograph independently fails to establish how dental conditions have progressed across multiple visits.

Missing Context

Image findings alone cannot represent lifestyle, oral-health habits, and symptoms vital for personalized care guidance.

Limitations & Future Work

Current Limitations

  • Different datasets utilized across individual research components.
  • Synthetic data reliance in the personalized assessment component.
  • DHS weights and risk thresholds are project-defined research measures.
  • Limited scope of supported dental diseases.
  • Potential failures in image registration under extreme variance.

Future Directions

  • Evaluation using a shared, ethically approved clinical cohort.
  • Clinical calibration of DHS weights and risk thresholds.
  • Expansion of disease categories and multi-population testing.
  • Usability studies involving dental professionals and patients.
